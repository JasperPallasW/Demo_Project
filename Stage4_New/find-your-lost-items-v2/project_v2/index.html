Questions

Why do I need an account?

So we know who reported an item, and so only logged in students can see the contact button. Finders also need an account to earn honors.

How do finders get recognition?

When a finder marks an item as returned, he or she gets 10 points. Badges: 1 return = Good Samaritan, 2 = Campus Helper, 3 = Campus Hero. Each returned item also has a printable certificate.

Why do you ask where I am from?

To show our diverse campus. Students from India choose their state, international students type their country. It is shown next to a name on the Heroes board.

Where is my data saved?

This is a front-end project: data is saved inside your own browser (localStorage). A real product would use a server and a database.
